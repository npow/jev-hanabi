# Multi-play signal clue pilot

This pilot used the corrected one-action clue criterion and forced only clues
that made at least two recipient slots guaranteed playable.

- Seeds 0–9, 10 games, 2-player self-play
- Mean score: **13.8/25**, scores 14, 13, 10, 14, 16, 15, 14, 13, 19, 10
- All 10 games retained all 3 lives

This is a pilot only and is not promoted over the corrected baseline (14.61/25
on seeds 10–109). A fresh paired expansion would be needed to evaluate the
signal forcing rule.
